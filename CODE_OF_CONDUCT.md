# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(version 2.1, licensed CC BY 4.0), in short form.

## Our standard

We want a friendly, harassment-free space for everybody, whatever their experience, background or identity. Please:

* be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux or to flashing devices;
* give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person;
* assume good faith, and say so when something is unclear instead of guessing;
* keep other people's personal data private — never post somebody else's identifiers, screenshots of their accounts or private messages.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal or political attacks, doxxing, sexualised language or attention,
and spam.

## Scope

This applies in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban people who repeat or seriously violate it. To report a problem, use "Report content" on the offending comment, or contact the
maintainers privately through the contact options on a maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
